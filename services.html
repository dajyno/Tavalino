What We Offer

Complete Renewable Energy Solutions

From power distribution and solar mini grids to engineering, lighting, and battery technology — we deliver turnkey EPC solutions for every scale.

Power Distribution & Energy Management

End-to-end power distribution solutions and intelligent energy management systems for residential, commercial, and industrial applications. From load analysis to commissioning, we deliver reliable power tailored to you.

Solar Mini Grid System

Community-scale solar mini grids delivering reliable, affordable electricity to neighbourhoods, estates, and rural communities. Custom-designed with smart load management for Nigerian conditions.

Engineering Services & Lighting

Professional electrical engineering design, installation, and solar lighting solutions for homes, businesses, and public spaces. From street lights to complete electrical system design.

Solar Street & Flood Lighting

High-performance solar street lights and flood lights for roads, compounds, car parks, security perimeters, and public spaces. Our all-in-one and split-type solar lighting systems feature automatic dusk-to-dawn operation, motion sensors, and weatherproof construction — delivering bright, reliable illumination without grid connection or running costs.

Solar Water Pumps

Solar-powered water pumping solutions for boreholes, irrigation, livestock watering, and community water supply. We design and install DC and AC solar pump systems with intelligent controllers, dry-run protection, and tank level automation — eliminating diesel costs and ensuring water access even in the most remote off-grid locations.

Battery Regeneration Workshop

Specialist battery regeneration and restoration services that extend the lifespan of lead-acid, gel, and deep-cycle batteries by up to 70%. Using advanced desulphation technology, we restore lost capacity in inverter, solar, and automotive batteries — saving you the cost of premature replacement and reducing battery waste.
