FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about going solar with Tavalino Energy.

How much does a solar system cost in Nigeria? +

Pricing depends on your energy needs and system size. A typical residential backup system starts at ₦1.2M, while a full off-grid solution ranges from ₦3.5M to ₦10M+. We provide a detailed, no-obligation quote after our free site assessment.

What warranty do you offer on installations? +

We offer industry-leading warranties: 25-year performance warranty on solar panels, up to 5 years on inverters, and 1–3 years on installation workmanship depending on your package. Battery warranties vary by type and are clearly explained during consultation.

How long does solar installation take? +

Most residential installations are completed within 1–5 days. Commercial and industrial projects vary based on scope and complexity. We provide a clear timeline during the design phase and work to meet or exceed it.

Do you offer maintenance after installation? +

Yes. We offer routine maintenance contracts that include panel cleaning, battery inspection, inverter servicing, and system performance monitoring — keeping your system at peak efficiency year-round.

What areas do you serve in Nigeria? +

Our offices are based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State,and FCT, Abuja and from there we serve clients across the entire country. Call us to discuss your location and project scope.
