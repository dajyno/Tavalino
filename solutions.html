Residential

Residential Solar Solutions

Power your home with clean, reliable solar energy. We design and install custom off-grid and hybrid systems for homes of all sizes — from compact backup units to full off-grid setups. Our residential solutions include high-efficiency solar panels, pure sine wave inverters, lithium battery storage, and smart energy monitoring, giving you uninterrupted power and freedom from expensive generator fuel.

  • Custom off-grid and hybrid system design
  • High-efficiency monocrystalline panels
  • Lithium iron phosphate battery storage
  • Smart energy monitoring via mobile app
  • Professional installation in 1–3 days
  • Full commissioning and user training
Commercial

Commercial Solar Solutions

Reduce operating costs and ensure business continuity with a tailored commercial solar system. We serve offices, retail spaces, hotels, hospitals, and educational institutions with systems ranging from 10KVA to 100KVA+. Our commercial installations feature intelligent load management, remote monitoring, and optional hybrid inverter configurations that seamlessly blend solar, battery, and grid power.

  • Systems from 10KVA to 100KVA+ capacity

  • Battery backup for business continuity

Industrial

Industrial Solar Solutions

Heavy-duty solar and energy storage systems engineered for factories, warehouses, agricultural operations, and large-scale facilities.   • Megawatt-scale PV array design & installation
  • Containerised lithium-ion battery storage
  • Mini-grid & island-mode infrastructure
