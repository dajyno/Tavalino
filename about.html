Who We Are

Experienced. Reliable. Renewable.

TAVALINO ENERGY has experience in the field of electrical engineering and renewable energy solutions. Our experienced staff and cross-disciplinary fields of application make TAVALINO ENERGY the partner that gives clients all the skill and support needed to manage and complete a project.

TAVALINO ENERGY operates a turnkey service, which includes comprehensive services such as consulting/power project, power management, feasibility study, renewable energy, plant installation, start up and commissioning, and all the necessary technical documentation. Each project follows a series of procedures that outline the responsibilities and action plan for the coordination and completion of duties related to the implementation of projects.

Tavalino Energy has wide ranges of partners in the renewable energy world — from off grid solutions to long time power back up and battery technology.
