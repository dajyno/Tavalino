Tavalino Energy Visits Zetatech Headquarters and Manufacturing Base, to Strengthen Strategic Expansion in Africa

Chengdu, China — ZETATECH recently welcomed representatives from Tavalino Energy to Chengdu for an in-depth visit to the company's headquarters and advanced energy storage manufacturing facilities, marking another significant milestone in the company's global market expansion.

Advanced Manufacturing Excellence Showcased

During the visit, our delegation toured ZETATECH's state-of-the-art production base, gaining firsthand insight into the company's comprehensive manufacturing capabilities across both commercial & industrial (C&I) and residential energy storage sectors. We also visited two C&I energy storage production lines and two residential storage production lines, where we observed critical manufacturing processes including OCV sorting, polarity testing, and End-of-Line (EOL) inspection, ensuring every product meets the highest international quality standards.

Our delegation also explored ZETATECH's 500-square-meter testing center, where all products undergo rigorous charge-discharge and performance validation before delivery. With over 120 residential energy storage testing stations and 20 C&I testing stations, ZETATECH's testing capacity fully supports large-scale daily production while maintaining exceptional product reliability.

Delegation touring ZETATECH's advanced manufacturing and testing facilities.

Comprehensive Product Portfolio and Utility-Scale Innovation

In the product exhibition area, we reviewed ZETATECH's flagship solutions, including the highly flexible and modular 261kWh battery cabinet and electrical cabinet system, which can be customized with PCS, MPPT, STS, and other modules according to project requirements. We also explored the MiniCub series, available in 80kWh and 96kWh versions, along with ZETATECH's diverse residential product portfolio, including wall-mounted batteries, rack-mounted batteries, and floor-standing systems.

A highlight of the visit was the tour of ZETATECH's new factory rooftop distributed solar project, featuring 1.6MW of photovoltaic generation paired with 3.4MWh of energy storage capacity, providing sustainable power support for factory operations. We also visited the company's advanced 5MWh containerized energy storage system, which integrates a 2.5MW power conversion and boosting unit with 2500kW PCS units and a 2500kVA transformer, demonstrating ZETATECH's robust capabilities in utility-scale energy solutions.

ZETATECH's 1.6MW rooftop solar project paired with 3.4MWh energy storage.
Reviewing ZETATECH's flagship energy storage solutions.

Corporate Strength and Smart Microgrid Leadership

At ZETATECH's headquarters, we toured the 800-square-meter corporate showroom, where we gained deeper understanding of the company's 20-year development journey, global market footprint, corporate culture, and three major business sectors: catalysts, energy storage, and composite materials.

ZETATECH also introduced its innovative microgrid solution, which combines solar, storage, and diesel generation in a highly reliable integrated architecture. This system is particularly suitable for islands, remote off-grid areas, and high-electricity-cost regions, offering both off-grid and grid-connected operational flexibility.

Exploring ZETATECH's 20-year journey at the corporate showroom.

Strategic Partnership Accelerates Africa Expansion

Following productive business discussions with company leadership, Tavalino Energy successfully signed a strategic cooperation agreement. This partnership further strengthens ZETATECH's presence in the African energy storage market and reinforces its commitment to delivering advanced, flexible, and reliable clean energy solutions worldwide.
