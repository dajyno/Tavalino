Tavalino Energy Services Signs Grant Agreement with World Bank on Deployment of Solar Home System

Abuja, Nigeria — Tavalino Energy Services has signed a grant agreement with the World Bank to deploy solar home systems across off-grid and underserved communities in Nigeria, marking a major milestone in the company's commitment to expanding energy access.

The agreement, formalised in August 2022, provides funding for the procurement, installation, and after-sales support of solar home systems targeting households and small businesses that currently lack reliable grid electricity. The initiative is part of the World Bank's broader renewable energy access programme aimed at reducing energy poverty in sub-Saharan Africa.

Scope of the Project

Under the grant, Tavalino Energy will deploy solar home systems consisting of solar panels, batteries, charge controllers, and energy-efficient DC appliances to eligible beneficiaries. The systems are designed to power lighting, phone charging, fans, and small entertainment devices — providing a meaningful improvement in quality of life for off-grid households.

  • Deployment of compact solar home systems to rural and peri-urban households
  • Training of local technicians for installation and maintenance
  • Establishment of community-based service centres for ongoing support
  • Monitoring and reporting framework to track impact and system performance
"This grant agreement is a testament to the confidence the World Bank has in Tavalino Energy's capacity to deliver last-mile energy solutions. We are proud to be part of this transformative initiative that brings clean, affordable electricity to Nigerian homes."

— Tavalino Energy Management
Signing the documents to power communities across Nigeria

Impact and Outlook

The project is expected to reach thousands of households across multiple states, with priority given to communities with the lowest grid connectivity rates. Beyond providing electricity, the initiative supports local economic activity — enabling small businesses to operate longer hours, improving access to information through phone charging, and enhancing safety with reliable lighting.

Tavalino Energy continues to work closely with the World Bank and local stakeholders to ensure the successful implementation and long-term sustainability of the programme.
